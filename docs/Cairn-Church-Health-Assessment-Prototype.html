The cost of not fixing it

This is why your giving looks weak.

You flagged generosity as a concern, and your instinct was a giving campaign. Here's what the chain shows: your generosity gap is a breadth problem — not enough people give — and breadth is downstream of belonging. People who don't feel known don't give. A campaign run into a connection gap raises money once and changes nothing by spring.

The same root is quietly capping your volunteer base. Strangers don't serve either.

The one next step

Give your outer ring an owner.

Before any new program, assign one person to own what happens to an attender in their first 90 days — capture, connect, and notice-when-they're-gone. Not a committee. One name. Everything else in this report waits behind that.
